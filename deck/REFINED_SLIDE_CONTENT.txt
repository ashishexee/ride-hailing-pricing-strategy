--- LEFT COLUMN (50%) ---
SECTION A: "Methodology Summary"
Text: "WS1 (Demand Suppression): Group-by analysis of cancellation, completion, satisfaction across surge bands, zone types, cities, and time windows (peak/off-peak, weekday/weekend). Negative margin heatmap across distance buckets × surge bands. Loss-leader hypothesis tested via correlation of negative CM with future ride frequency (r=-0.324, p<0.001 — rejected). Revenue concentration: 62.8% of CM from 31.2% surge trips.

WS2 (Price Elasticity): Arc elasticity = Δlog(completed_demand) / Δlog(fare_per_km) between adjacent surge bands. Completion baseline adjusted from 100% (data artifact at 1.0x) to 97% for all analysis. Validated value (0.22) from controlled regression with time-of-day, zone, and weather fixed effects. Weather confounding tested and found NEGLIGIBLE (precip-surge r=-0.004). Non-monotonic completion at 1.75x (61.8% of subgroups) documented as data artifact.

WS3 (Unit Economics): DCF LTV with 18-month horizon, 10% annual discount rate. Churn model: bracketed satisfaction-to-annual-churn mapping from industry benchmarks. Breakeven churn computed via binary search (50 iterations, monthly churn where DCF LTV = CAC). NRR computed as revenue-weighted (retained customer revenue / total customer revenue).

WS4 (Scenario Simulation): Trip-level methodology. For each capped trip: new fare = fare × (capped_surge / actual_surge). New CM = new_revenue - cost_per_trip (conservative: fixed cost). New completion from adjusted completion curve at capped surge. Satisfaction from interpolation at capped surge. Demand elasticity effect: demand_increase = |elasticity| × price_reduction%. Churn improvement: customer-level new satisfaction → new annual churn → new LTV. Status Quo (S0) returns 0.00% by definition."
Font: Regular, 9pt, charcoal. Key terms in Bold.

SECTION B: "Data Quality Caveats" — Bold, 12pt, alert red
Text: "1. Completion at 1.0x surge = 100% — impossible in real ride-hailing. Adjusted to 97% baseline for all analysis.
2. Non-monotonic completion at 1.75x (0.913 > 0.904 at 1.5x) — widespread data artifact (61.8% of subgroups). Interpolation used in simulation.
3. 37.1% of loyalty_tier is NULL (imputed as 'Unknown'). Option 3 only protects Gold+Silver (30.1% of trips). Unknown tier lacks any protection.
4. Churn model (satisfaction→annual_churn) is NOT validated in this dataset. Behavioral proxy correlations: days_since r=-0.045, risk_flag r=-0.025, ride_freq r=0.024. All essentially zero. Churn improvement magnitude is UNCERTAIN.
5. No causal claims — all simulation results are projection-based under modeled assumptions. No A/B experiment was conducted."
Font: Regular, 9pt, charcoal.
